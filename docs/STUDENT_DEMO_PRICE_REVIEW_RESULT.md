# Student demo pricing review

5 November 2026. The user noted that the demonstration catalogue was too expensive
for a mainly student audience. This concern is valid: the older 24 demos copied
commercial short-term asking rates, and the 40 new coverage examples used labelled
but unresearched sample amounts. Neither is proof of student-market accuracy.

## Checked corrections applied

Read the publisher's live [FFCAMERA price list](https://ffcamera.my/price-list/),
including its explicit **1day / 2day** headings. Use the first amount only; do not
divide the two-day total and call it an advertised one-day price.

| Existing demo model | Previous daily amount | Revised one-day amount |
|---|---:|---:|
| Canon 5D Mark III | RM180 | RM150 |
| Canon EOS R6 Mark II | RM350 | RM240 |
| Sony A7 III | RM210 | RM180 |
| Sony A7S III | RM450 | RM380 |

Applied to eight matching body-only demos across the two existing Owners. Product
identity, condition, age, package, images, deposits and delivery charges were
preserved. Descriptions now identify the revised commercial source and review
date. These are asking-price examples, not achieved rental prices, discounts
guaranteed by the publisher or a validated student-market average.

Only the corresponding demo listing fields changed. All account/listing/history
counts stayed the same. Hash comparisons of saved booking charges/status and
payment amounts/status also remained identical. Existing agreed booking prices
were not repriced. Private before/after backups and receipts are under
`services/api/.data/evidence/category-demo-coverage-2026-11-05/`.

## Why this does not complete student-market pricing

Some expensive models remain genuinely premium products. Cheaper quotations for
another camera generation, a different package, another country or a long-term
contract cannot justify changing their daily rate. For example, a quote for an
R5 Mark II does not establish the rate for the original R5. Monthly laptop leases
were not converted into available one-day rentals. Search snippets for S23 Ultra
were not treated as quotes for the base S23.

The catalogue also needs a budget-oriented product mix rather than invented low
rates for premium items. Reviewed candidates include:

- Canon 700D: RM35 for one day on the FFCAMERA list.
- Canon 60D: RM45 for one day on the same list.
- Canon R100 body: RM50 for one day on the same list; the lens package is separate.
- Perodua Axia: RM110/day for 1–7 days on the
  [DRIVOR individual vehicle page](https://drivor.my/vehicle/perodua-axia/).
  Its lower headline minimum applies to longer durations and was not used.

These alternatives are prepared for review; they have not replaced existing
products or photos. The user was asked whether to retain the current models or
replace some premium demonstration items with budget options. That selection was
not yet received at the time of this report.

The remaining unresearched sample amounts, example deposits and delivery fees
must remain labelled as demonstration choices. A full, independently supported
student-price review is **not complete**. No general discount factor, category
price cap or fixed brand-price rule was introduced.

## Boundaries and verification

- All 64 demos remain `sourceType = demo_seed`, with unverified price provenance;
  none becomes clean pricing evidence or real rental history.
- No publisher was enabled, scraped automatically or imported into the live
  pricing-reference collection. No AI model, feature, pricing calculation or
  recommendation ranking was changed by these listing-price corrections.
- The public API returned 67 unique active records across both pages, including
  all 64 demos. All eight revised camera entries showed the intended amounts.
- Full physical-category coverage and both service examples remain intact.
- Source facts were reviewed through public pages; no source photographs or
  private marketplace seller/customer data were copied.
